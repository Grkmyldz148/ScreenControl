Control your displays and volume from your phone.

Turn on **Control from phone** in the gear menu, press **Show QR** in the panel,
and scan it with your phone's camera. The page that opens has a slider for every
display, Off/25/50/100% buttons, the link toggle and the Mac's volume. It works in
any phone browser — Android or iPhone — with nothing to install. Add it to your
home screen to keep it one tap away.

- Only devices on your own network (or Tailscale) can connect, and every request
  has to carry the secret from the QR code. **New link** revokes it.
- Volume follows the output device when macOS can set its level. For audio
  interfaces with a hardware knob, the page offers − / Mute / + buttons that press
  the volume keys, so apps like SoundSource pick them up.
- Automation apps such as HTTP Shortcuts can use the same API; see the README.

The panel now always opens directly below the menu bar, on the screen whose icon
you clicked. With a monitor arranged above the laptop it used to open half
off-screen, and it no longer slides away with an auto-hiding menu bar.
