First public release.

- Per-display brightness sliders for the built-in panel and DDC/CI monitors.
- `F1`/`F2` drive both displays together through a calibration curve you teach it.
- True black on external monitors: gamma dimming below DDC zero, then backlight off.
- On-screen HUD, hot-plug and sleep handling, `--diagnose` troubleshooting mode.
- Signed and notarized, with automatic updates.
